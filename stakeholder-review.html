Tranh Kim Hoàng

A game-based digital drawing app for children aged 5–8 with motor disabilities — teaching a nearly-lost Vietnamese folk art tradition through tap-only, AI-assisted interaction.

📱 Web App (React + TypeScript) 🎨 Cultural × Accessibility 🏫 High School Portfolio Project 🚫 No Backend · No App Store
Overview

Executive Summary

What we're building: A single-page React app that lets children aged 5–8 with motor disabilities experience Kim Hoàng Vietnamese folk painting through a 7-screen, tap-only flow — choose paper color → stamp a woodblock outline → color zones → add a poem character → save the result as a PNG.
🎯

The Core Bet

One tap = one meaningful creative action. No drag, no precision grip, no sustained pressure — just intention → result.

🏛️

Cultural Mission

Kim Hoàng painting is nearly extinct (last practised 1945, restored 2015). This app introduces a new generation to the authentic technique.

✅

MVP Deliverable

All 7 screens navigable. Pig motif fully functional. Rule-based AI color suggestions. Save as PNG. No backend required.

Cultural Foundation

What Is Kim Hoàng Painting?

Tranh Kim Hoàng is an 18th-century Vietnamese woodcut folk art from Kim Hoàng village, Hoài Đức, Hanoi. Understanding the tradition is essential to understanding every design decision in this app.

1

Pre-dye the paper

Paper is dyed scarlet red (giấy hồng điều) or Chinese yellow (giấy vàng tầu) before anything else. The background carries emotional weight.

2

Stamp the woodblock

A carved jackfruit wood block is inked and pressed onto the paper — creating the bold black outline in one decisive action.

3

Hand-color zones

Mineral-based pigments (vermilion, gardenia yellow, white gypsum, indigo) are applied by hand with straw brushes. Same template, unique coloring every time.

4

Write the poem

A Chinese character (福 Phúc, 祿 Lộc, 壽 Thọ) is handwritten in the top-left corner in cursive chữ thảo script — image + word = complete meaning.

History

Nearly Lost

A 1915 flood destroyed most original woodblocks in Kim Hoàng village. By 1945, no families continued the practice. Restoration efforts by collector Nguyễn Thị Thu Hoa began in 2015, recovering ~80 surviving paintings. The app participates in this cultural continuity.

Technique

Why the Pig?

The pig (lợn) is the most iconic Kim Hoàng motif — a Tết symbol of prosperity. The fat, round pig with a curled tail is drawn in a characteristic folk style: bold black outline, chunky proportions, flat mineral colors. It is the MVP's primary motif.

Problem & Opportunity

Who Is Excluded and Why

Traditional art activities — including village visits to Kim Hoàng — assume a set of motor capabilities that many children simply don't have.

The exclusion barrier: Holding a brush, staying inside lines, applying consistent pressure, and using bilateral hand coordination are all required by traditional painting. Children with cerebral palsy, developmental coordination disorder (DCD), or upper-limb differences are systematically excluded from the creative experience.
🧒

Primary User

  • Age 5–8
  • Motor disability (tremors, limited fine-motor control, single-finger tap only)
  • May have co-occurring attention differences
  • Likely uses a tablet with a caregiver nearby
👩‍🏫

Secondary User (Facilitator)

  • Parent, teacher, or therapist
  • Sets up the app before handing it to the child
  • May toggle caregiver settings (sound, mode)
  • Sits alongside but does not control the session
🚫

Design Non-Goals

  • No drag interactions
  • No voice control (co-occurring speech impairments)
  • No time pressure or countdowns
  • No "wrong answer" states
  • No login or accounts
Research backing: Design constraints derive from WHO Global Report on Assistive Technology (2021), UNICEF "Designing for Children with Disabilities" guidelines, and Universal Design for Learning (UDL) Framework. See References for full citations.

Intelligence

AI Assist System — Three Layers

The "AI" in this app is intentionally rule-based for MVP — no live model calls, no API dependency. This is a deliberate accessibility design choice: intelligent defaults, not a language model.

1

Zone Snap Tolerance ✅ MVP

Children with tremors or imprecision aim for a zone and may land 50px off-center. The system finds the nearest zone center within an 80px snap radius and credits the tap to that zone. If no zone is within range, a gentle canvas shake plays — no error text, no red X. The snap radius can be increased to 120px for higher accessibility needs.

2

Color Suggestion Engine ✅ MVP

In Guided Mode, the app automatically selects the historically accurate Kim Hoàng color for each zone — derived from research on real 18th-century paintings. The child taps a zone and the correct color floods in without needing to make a palette choice. In Free Mode, the child manually selects a color first. Completion is detected when every zone in the motif's color map has been filled.

3

Stroke Completion ❌ V2 Only

Higher-functioning children submit a rough scribble; a fine-tuned vision model (ControlNet scribble mode or Claude API with vision) returns a Kim Hoàng-style SVG outline. Out of scope for MVP due to API latency, cost, and portfolio demo constraints. Documented as the full product vision.

Inclusive Design

Accessibility Commitments

Every constraint in this app is an accessibility decision. These are non-negotiable requirements, not polish.

👆

Tap Only — No Drag

Zero drag interactions anywhere in the app. Children with tremors or limited grip cannot reliably drag. One physical gesture = one creative action.

📏

80px Minimum Tap Targets

Every tappable element is at least 80×80px. Most are significantly larger (the stamp canvas is the entire screen). Enforced in every component.

⏱️

No Time Pressure

No countdowns, no timeouts, no loading bars that run out. The child can take as long as they need on every screen.

✅

Every Tap Produces a Result

Nothing silently fails. A tap on a zone produces a fill. A miss produces a shake. The stamp tap produces an animation. Failure is always soft and visual.

🎨

Color + Shape, Never Color Alone

No information is conveyed by color only. Every color swatch has a Vietnamese label. Every zone has a shape. Text contrast ≥ 4.5:1 on all backgrounds.

🇻🇳

Bilingual (Vietnamese + English)

Every user-facing string has Vietnamese primary label and English secondary. Uses Be Vietnam Pro — the only major sans-serif optimized for Vietnamese diacritics.

↩️

Undo Always Visible

One large undo button, always visible during coloring. Maintains a stack of zone fills. Children can explore without fear of ruining their work.

✨

Reduced Motion Support

All animations respect prefers-reduced-motion. When enabled, transitions collapse to instant state changes. No flashing or strobing effects anywhere.

🏆

Success Is Guaranteed

Guided Mode's color suggestions ensure every child produces an authentically Kim Hoàng painting — regardless of color knowledge or decision-making ability.
